Models
------
Model weights are downloaded separately and are not part of the llama.cpp
runtime. Each model's licence and attribution are recorded in the installed
module metadata and are shown in the AI Module panel. Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-
Instruct and Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B-Instruct are published by the Qwen project
under Apache-2.0. Apache-2.0 permits commercial use and redistribution when
the licence and attribution notices are retained.

GisCode does not send prompts, source code, conversations or project files to
a cloud AI service. Inference runs in a local llama-server process bound to
127.0.0.1.
